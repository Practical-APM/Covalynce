Version: 1.0

# Executive Summary

OpenCost AI is an AI FinOps and Governance platform that enables organizations to understand, control, optimize, and govern AI spending across all AI providers, applications, and agents.

As enterprises increasingly adopt AI, spending is becoming fragmented across:

* OpenAI
* Anthropic
* Google Gemini
* Azure OpenAI
* AWS Bedrock
* Cursor
* Windsurf
* GitHub Copilot
* Internal AI Agents

Organizations currently lack:

* Spend visibility
* Budget controls
* Cost attribution
* ROI measurement
* Governance policies

OpenCost AI aims to become the control plane through which all enterprise AI traffic flows.

---

# Problem Statement

Today's organizations cannot answer basic questions regarding AI usage.

Examples:

* How much did we spend on AI last month?
* Which teams are spending the most?
* Which AI tools generate value?
* Which AI agents are consuming the most tokens?
* Which users are responsible for unexpected costs?
* Which models should we replace with cheaper alternatives?

The absence of visibility and governance creates:

* Budget overruns
* Unpredictable costs
* Shadow AI usage
* Security risks
* Compliance concerns
* Lack of ROI measurement
