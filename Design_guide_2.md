Brand Personality & Messaging Constitution

Mission

Communicate complex technology in a way that feels:

- Intelligent
- Clear
- Credible
- Human
- Outcome-oriented

The website should never sound like marketing.

It should sound like experts explaining a valuable product.

---

Brand Voice

We Are

- Confident
- Precise
- Helpful
- Rational
- Transparent

---

We Are Not

- Hype-driven
- Buzzword-heavy
- Corporate
- Salesy
- Overly technical

---

Writing Principles

Clarity Over Cleverness

Bad:

"The future of autonomous intelligence."

Good:

"AI agents that resolve customer tickets automatically."

Users should understand the product immediately.

---

Outcomes Over Features

Bad:

"Powered by advanced multi-agent orchestration."

Good:

"Reduce support workload by 60%."

Focus on business impact.

---

Specificity Over Abstraction

Bad:

"Transform your organization."

Good:

"Generate product requirements directly from customer feedback."

Specific language builds trust.

---

Show Evidence

Every important claim should be supported by:

- Data
- Examples
- Screenshots
- Testimonials
- Case studies

Never ask users to trust unsupported claims.

---

Homepage Messaging Formula

Hero

Structure:

[Outcome]

for

[Target User]

without

[Current Pain]

Example:

"Turn customer feedback into prioritized product decisions without manually reviewing thousands of conversations."

---

Problem Section

Describe the user's existing workflow.

Make them recognize themselves.

The visitor should think:

"That's exactly my problem."

---

Solution Section

Explain:

1. Input
2. Processing
3. Output
4. Outcome

Simple systems are trusted systems.

---

Benefits Section

Every benefit should follow:

Feature → Capability → Business Outcome

Example:

"Conversation clustering"

→ Groups similar feedback

→ Helps teams identify the highest-impact opportunities faster

---

Case Studies

Always include:

- Situation
- Action
- Result

Prefer numbers over adjectives.

Bad:

"Greatly improved productivity."

Good:

"Reduced analysis time from 12 hours to 45 minutes."

---

Headline Rules

Headlines should be:

- Short
- Clear
- Outcome-focused

Maximum ideal length:

4–10 words

Examples:

- Understand Customers Faster
- Build What Users Actually Need
- Turn Feedback Into Decisions
- Product Intelligence That Scales

Avoid:

- Revolutionize
- Transform
- Unlock
- Empower
- Reimagine
- Next-generation

These words are usually empty.

---

CTA Rules

Good CTAs:

- Start Free
- Book Demo
- Try It Now
- See It In Action

Avoid:

- Learn More
- Discover
- Explore
- Get Started Today

The action should be explicit.

---

Product Presentation

Whenever explaining a feature:

1. What it does
2. Why it matters
3. What outcome it creates

Never stop at feature descriptions.

---

Trust Framework

Trust should be established using:

- Customer logos
- Security credentials
- Usage metrics
- Product screenshots
- Testimonials
- Case studies

Never rely on self-praise.

---

AI Messaging Rules

Do not lead with AI.

Lead with the problem.

Users buy outcomes, not AI.

Bad:

"AI-powered customer intelligence."

Good:

"Find the product opportunities hidden inside customer conversations."

AI should be the engine, not the headline.

---

Final Review Checklist

Before publishing any page:

- Can a visitor explain the product in one sentence after 10 seconds?
- Does every section focus on user value?
- Is every claim supported by evidence?
- Are outcomes more visible than features?
- Is the copy free from buzzwords?
- Would a skeptical buyer trust this page?

If not, rewrite the section.
