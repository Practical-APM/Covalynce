# Design Anti-Patterns

Never generate:

- Generic AI gradients
- Floating abstract spheres
- Random 3D objects
- Glassmorphism everywhere
- Neon blue on black
- Overuse of blur effects
- Marketing buzzwords
- Long paragraphs
- Feature walls
- Multiple competing CTAs
- Excessive animations
- Fake dashboards
- Stock photography
- Generic AI illustrations
- Empty claims without evidence

Avoid making the website look like:

- Every YC AI startup
- A crypto landing page
- A Web3 project
- A design showcase with no product

Prioritize:

- Product clarity
- Trust
- Business outcomes
- Elegant typography
- Structured information hierarchy
- Conversion
